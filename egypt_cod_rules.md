# Egypt COD Rules — Market Specifics & Operating Framework

## KPIs — The Numbers That Matter

| Metric | Target | Warning | Kill |
|--------|--------|---------|------|
| CPL (Cost Per Lead) | 15–30 EGP | 31–45 EGP | > 45 EGP |
| ROAS | ≥ 1.8 | 1.3–1.79 | < 1.3 |
| Confirm Rate | 50–65% | 40–49% | < 40% |
| CTR (Link Click) | ≥ 1.5% | 0.8–1.4% | < 0.8% |
| CPM | 15–40 EGP | 41–60 EGP | > 60 EGP |

**ROAS calculation for Egypt COD:**
```
ROAS = (Orders confirmed × Avg order value) ÷ Ad spend
Note: Use CONFIRMED orders, not total leads. Confirm rate is the critical multiplier.
```

## Target Audience

### Primary: Egyptian Women 23–45
- **Location focus:** Tier 2–3 cities outperform Cairo for COD — Alexandria, Mansoura, Tanta, Assiut, Zagazig, Sohag, Beni Suef
- **Device:** 95%+ mobile, primarily Android
- **Connectivity:** 4G/LTE standard; avoid heavy video files > 30MB
- **Shopping behavior:** Impulse-driven, price-sensitive, community-influenced (WhatsApp groups, Facebook groups)
- **Trust threshold:** Higher than average — they need multiple reassurances before ordering

### Secondary: Egyptian Women 18–22 (younger, trend-sensitive)
- More responsive to social proof and influencer-style UGC
- Lower AOV but higher impulse rate

### Audience building priority:
1. Lookalike from confirmed orders (best signal)
2. Lookalike from add-to-cart / initiate checkout
3. Interest targeting: women's fashion, beauty, home, baby products, cooking
4. Broad (age + gender only) — works well when pixel is trained

## COD Trust Signals — Mandatory

Every creative and landing page MUST include at minimum 3 of these 5:

| Signal | Arabic Text | Placement |
|--------|-------------|-----------|
| Cash on delivery | "الدفع عند الاستلام" / "كاش عند باب بيتك" | Hook or end card |
| Free shipping | "توصيل مجاني لكل المحافظات" | Prominent overlay |
| Return guarantee | "ضمان الاسترجاع 14 يوم" | End card or caption |
| Price upfront | "بـ [X] جنيه بس" | First 5 seconds |
| Delivery time | "هتوصلك في 2-5 أيام بس" | Middle or end |

**Trust signal hierarchy:** COD > Free shipping > Price > Delivery time > Return policy

## Forbidden Patterns — Automatic Disqualifiers

### Product disqualifiers:
- Ships from China with 14+ day delivery → kills confirm rate, triggers chargebacks
- Requires app download to order
- Has no local Egyptian supplier/warehouse
- Price > 500 EGP for unknown brands (high hesitation, low confirm rate)
- Requires size selection without a clear size guide in Arabic

### Ad copy disqualifiers:
- Discount claims > 70% off ("تخفيض 80%") — feels like a scam to Egyptian buyers
- "أضف إلى السلة" / "Buy Now" / "Shop Now" language implying card payment
- Dollar or euro pricing
- Foreign brand prominence (if not already trusted in Egypt)
- "Limited time" claims that run for weeks — destroys credibility

### Visual disqualifiers:
- Models who are clearly not Egyptian (skin tone, style, background)
- Backgrounds that scream "China warehouse"
- Products shown without actual usage in Egyptian homes
- No Arabic text visible on screen for video ads

## Meta Platform Rules for Egypt

### Placement performance (ranked):
1. **Reels** — Highest reach, lowest CPM, best for UGC-style creatives
2. **Feed (Facebook)** — Best for static ads, carousels, slightly older audience
3. **Stories** — Good for urgency creatives, short (<15 sec) content
4. **Marketplace** — Works for home goods, furniture, kitchen products

### Budget guidelines:
| Phase | Daily Budget | Duration | Decision |
|-------|-------------|----------|----------|
| Testing | 50–100 EGP/day | 3–4 days | Kill or continue based on CPL |
| Validation | 100–200 EGP/day | 3–7 days | Scale or optimize based on ROAS |
| Scaling | 200–500 EGP/day | Ongoing | Monitor frequency and CPL creep |

### Campaign structure (proven for Egypt COD):
- **Campaign objective:** Leads (not conversions — COD is phone-confirmed, not digital checkout)
- **Ad set:** 1 audience per ad set (avoid stacking audiences)
- **Ads per ad set:** 3–5 creatives max (Meta needs variety to optimize)
- **Bid strategy:** Lowest cost (don't use cost cap until you have historical data)

### Frequency management:
- Frequency > 3.0 → start creative refresh
- Frequency > 5.0 → kill ad set, refresh audience

## Product Selection Rules

### Green light (COD-friendly products):
- Solves a daily pain for Egyptian women (cleaning, beauty, cooking, baby care)
- Price point: 150–400 EGP sweet spot
- Ships from Egypt: 2–5 day delivery
- Has visible "wow factor" that works on video
- Can be demonstrated in 15–30 seconds
- Has no size/fit risk (or very clear size guide)

### Red light (COD killers):
- Electronics > 600 EGP (payment hesitation)
- Fashion with complex sizing (high return rate)
- Any product shipped internationally
- Products that require explanation > 60 seconds
- Supplements and health claims (Meta policy issues + low trust)
- Products already saturated in Egypt market (check Facebook Ad Library)

### Category performance (Egypt 2024–2025):
| Category | COD Performance | Notes |
|----------|----------------|-------|

## Confirm Rate Optimization

Confirm rate = % of leads who answer the call and confirm the order.

**Confirm rate killers:**
- Misleading ad (product looks different than described)
- Price surprise at confirmation (hidden fees)
- Long delivery time (> 7 days)
- Calling too late (> 24 hours after lead)
- Calling at bad times (before 9 AM, after 10 PM, during prayer times)

**Confirm rate boosters:**
- Call within 2 hours of lead
- Call between 10 AM–1 PM or 6–9 PM
- Short, clear order confirmation script in Egyptian dialect
- Reconfirm the COD/free shipping offer during the call
- Have the exact product description ready to answer questions

**Benchmark by product type:**
- Home goods: 55–65%
- Beauty: 50–60%
- Fashion: 45–55%
- Electronics: 40–50%
