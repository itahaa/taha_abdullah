# Scoring Rubric — Creative Hunter Egypt COD

Score each creative on 5 dimensions. Each dimension is 0–5. Maximum total = 25.

---

## Dimension 1: Hook (0–5)

Does it stop the scroll in the first 1–3 seconds?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **0** | No hook whatsoever. Opens on a logo, brand name, or product shot with no emotional trigger. Viewer scrolls past in under 1 second. |
| **1** | Very generic hook ("هل تعانين من...؟" with no specificity). Weak attempt that fails to create tension. |
| **2** | Recognizable hook structure but execution is flat. Mild scroll-stop potential. Forgettable within 5 seconds. |
| **3** | Decent hook. Clear problem/curiosity/demo opener. Stops some scrollers but won't consistently grab cold traffic. |
| **4** | Strong hook. Clear emotional pull — pain, curiosity, or desire. First 3 seconds make the viewer want to watch more. |
| **5** | Irresistible. Pattern interrupt + emotional trigger. Egyptian women aged 23–45 will stop, lean in, and watch. Could be a bold claim, a shocking visual, a relatable problem shown viscerally. |

**Hook types to recognize:**
- **Problem-agitation:** Opens showing the pain directly
- **Curiosity gap:** Teases a surprising result or secret
- **Demo:** Immediate product-in-action, dramatic before/after
- **Transformation:** Shows the outcome first, then reveals how
- **Social proof:** "X نساء جربوها وقالوا..."
- **Urgency:** Time/quantity scarcity that feels real

---

## Dimension 2: Production (0–5)

Video quality, editing, captions, and viewer experience.

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **0** | Completely unwatchable. Blurry, shaky, no audio sync, no subtitles. Would embarrass the brand. |
| **1** | Poor quality. One major flaw (no captions, terrible lighting, echo audio). Distracts from message. |
| **2** | Acceptable but amateur. Multiple minor flaws. Watchable but looks cheap. May hurt brand trust. |
| **3** | Decent production. Clean enough. Subtitles present. Pacing reasonable. Passes the "won't embarrass" test. |
| **4** | Clean and professional. Good lighting, clear audio, on-screen text at key moments, smooth CapCut editing. Feels local and authentic. |
| **5** | Excellent. Either high-quality UGC (real person, natural lighting, conversational) or polished studio feel. Subtitles synced, branding consistent, pacing keeps attention for full duration. |

**Production checklist:**
- [ ] Subtitles/captions visible (mandatory for Egyptian mobile feed)
- [ ] Audio clear (no echo, background noise)
- [ ] Lighting adequate (face visible if UGC)
- [ ] Text overlays at key selling points
- [ ] Duration appropriate (15–45 sec for COD products)
- [ ] Aspect ratio correct (9:16 for Reels/Stories, 1:1 for Feed)

## Dimension 3: COD Fit (0–5)

How well does this creative align with Egyptian COD buying psychology?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **0** | No COD signals at all. Asks for prepayment or card. Looks foreign. Would destroy trust with Egyptian audience. |
| **1** | Mentions price somewhere but no explicit trust signals. Audience has no reason to believe it's safe. |
| **2** | Has one trust signal (e.g., "كاش عند الاستلام") but feels generic/foreign. Missing local cues. |
| **3** | Has COD mention OR free shipping clearly stated. Feels local. Price is visible somewhere. |
| **4** | Has COD + free shipping + return guarantee. Price is prominent. Feels Egyptian-made. |
| **5** | All trust signals present (COD badge, free shipping, return policy, Egyptian phone number or WhatsApp). Price shown upfront ("بـ X جنيه بس"). Delivery promise (2–5 days). Feels 100% local and safe. |

**Egyptian COD trust signals (memorize these):**
- ✅ "الدفع عند الاستلام" / "كاش عند الباب"
- ✅ "توصيل مجاني"
- ✅ "ضمان الاسترجاع 14 يوم"
- ✅ Price shown clearly and early
- ✅ Delivery promise: "هتوصلك في 2-5 أيام"
- ✅ Egyptian phone number or WhatsApp CTA
- ✅ Arabic dialect throughout (not MSA, not English)

**COD killers (automatic deduction):**
- ❌ "أضف إلى السلة" / "ادفع الآن" (online payment implied)
- ❌ Dollar or Euro pricing
- ❌ "شحن دولي" or international shipping mention
- ❌ Foreign brand name/logo prominently featured
- ❌ Claims of >70% discount (too-good-to-be-true)

## Dimension 4: Longevity (0–5)

How long can this creative run without burning out or becoming irrelevant?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **0** | Tied to a specific date, news event, or trend that is already passing. Will be dead in days. |
| **1** | Trend-dependent. Will work for 1–2 weeks max then feel dated. |
| **2** | Neutral but has elements (specific celebrity, seasonal reference) that limit its shelf life to ~2 weeks. |
| **3** | Could run 3–4 weeks. No hard expiry but may fatigue with repeated exposure. |
| **4** | Evergreen concept. Could run 30–45 days before creative fatigue sets in. No date/trend references. |
| **5** | Timeless. The hook, problem, and solution are permanently relevant to the target audience. Could run for 60+ days with proper frequency capping. Works in any season. |

**Longevity killers:**
- References to current events or news
- Ramadan/Eid specific copy (unless during that season)
- Specific celebrity or influencer who may fall out of favor
- "العرض ينتهي اليوم" if the ad will run for weeks (kills credibility)
- Seasonal products shown out of season

## Dimension 5: Engagement (0–5)

Does this creative make people comment, save, or share?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **0** | Nothing in the creative invites any engagement. Pure broadcast, no conversation trigger. |
| **1** | Low engagement signal. Maybe a generic "شاركي مع صديقاتك" but no real hook to act on. |
| **2** | Some comment bait (a question or relatable situation) but it feels forced. Low organic engagement expected. |
| **3** | One clear engagement trigger — either a question that makes women tag friends, OR a save-worthy tip/list. |
| **4** | Strong comment bait (relatable problem that makes women say "أنا كمان كده") OR strong save trigger (useful information). |
| **5** | Both: a comment trigger (tagging, agreeing, sharing experience) AND a save/share trigger (useful, funny, or emotionally resonant enough to forward to friends). Comments will run, lowering CPM. |

**Engagement triggers that work for Egyptian women:**
- **Tag bait:** "وريها لصاحبتك اللي بتحتاجها" / "بعتيلك عشان فاكراكي"
- **Relate bait:** A situation so common women comment "أنا كمان بعاني من ده"
- **Poll/question:** "إيه رأيك؟" or "جربتي قبل كده؟"
- **Save trigger:** A tip, recipe, or hack they'll want to reference later
- **Share trigger:** Something funny or surprising enough to send to a friend

## Score Interpretation

| Total | Verdict | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| **20–25** | ✓ Test it | Launch immediately. Budget: 50–100 EGP/day to start. |
| **15–19** | ~ Maybe | Identify the weakest dimension. Can it be fixed before launch? If yes, fix and re-score. If no, skip. |
| **< 15** | ✗ Skip | Do not test. Opportunity cost is too high. Move to the next creative. |

**Hard rule:** Never test a creative below 15/25, no exceptions.
